## 5. Legacy Config Behavior
VLESS configs containing `connection_pool` field are now REJECTED.

Mechanism: json.Decoder.DisallowUnknownFields() causes decode error when unknown
field "connection_pool" is encountered.

Error message contains the string "connection_pool" for identification.

Two tests verify this behavior:
- TestLegacyConnectionPoolRejected
- TestLegacyConnectionPoolRejectedMinimal
